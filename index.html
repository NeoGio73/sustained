How it plays

Each line of testimony is live for a few seconds. Hit OBJECTION! and pick a ground. The right ground gets Sustained and points. The wrong ground gets Overruled and costs the judge's patience. Objecting to a proper question is frivolous, and the judge remembers. Let bad evidence in and the gallery (the GAL, the case manager, the bailiff) glazes over while the record fills with things you'll regret on appeal.

Three sustained in a row and Mr. Pruitt starts to sweat. Remember: leading is fair game on cross, so save that objection for his own witnesses. Keep the judge's patience above zero or you are in contempt.
