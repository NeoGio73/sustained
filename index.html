How it plays

Each line of testimony is live for a few seconds. Hit OBJECTION! and pick a ground. The right ground gets Sustained and points. The wrong ground gets Overruled and costs the judge's patience. Objecting to a perfectly good question is frivolous, and the judge remembers. Let a bad question slide and the jury dozes off.

Three sustained in a row and opposing counsel starts to sweat. Keep the judge's patience above zero or you are in contempt.
